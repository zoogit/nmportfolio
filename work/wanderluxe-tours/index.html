WanderLuxe Tours

Building a Luxury Travel Brand from the Ground Up

I develop the visual identity, website design, and digital experience for WanderLuxe Tours, positioning the company as a premium provider of bespoke travel experiences in Egypt.

Role
Brand Designer, Web Designer, UI/UX Designer, Developer
Type
Brand identity, website design, UI/UX, responsive web

Deliverables

Logo design, brand identity system, color palette and typography, website design, tour page templates, responsive layouts, content structure, and inquiry forms.

The Challenge

WanderLuxe came to me with nothing but a concept: bespoke luxury travel in Egypt. No logo, no brand, no visual language. The task was to build an identity from scratch that could credibly compete in a premium market, where trust and visual sophistication are prerequisites before a client will even consider booking.

My Approach

I led every creative decision from the ground up and executed the whole thing solo: brand strategy, visual identity, and full website design and build. The direction had to feel genuinely luxurious without turning cold, and culturally grounded without being decorative.

Results

The brand and site launched, remain live and active, and have driven a large increase in bookings, turning inquiries into trips and giving WanderLuxe a credible premium presence in the luxury travel space.
