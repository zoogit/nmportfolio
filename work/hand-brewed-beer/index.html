Hand Brewed Beer

Building a Craft Brewery Brand Across Packaging and Campaigns

I design labels, merchandise, outdoor advertising, and marketing materials to help Hand Brewed Beer strengthen its visual identity and connect with customers.

Role
Graphic Designer and Brand Partner
Type
Packaging, merchandise, advertising, brand support

Deliverables

Beer can labels, packaging design, billboard campaigns, event posters, merchandise, social media graphics, and promotional materials.

The Challenge

Hand Brewed came to me with a great product and no way to show it. The task was a brand that could work everywhere at once, on a can, a billboard, a t-shirt, and a phone screen, staying cohesive and genuinely inviting rather than falling into generic craft-brewery cliche.

My Approach

I built the full brand from scratch: visual identity, tone, and a system flexible enough to stretch across every format. From there I executed across packaging, merchandise, outdoor, event, and digital touchpoints, holding one consistent voice while letting each format breathe on its own terms.

Results

A relationship now past ten years and still active, the kind a brewery only renews when the work keeps performing. Across that decade I have built and maintained the brand ecosystem across can labels, apparel, billboard campaigns, event posters, and social assets.
