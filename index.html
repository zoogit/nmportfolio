Design Manager

Leading a Global Presentation Design Team in a High-Pressure Financial Environment

I now run the LA office presentation design team in a global investment banking environment, overseeing quality, mentoring creatives, and implementing workflow improvements that help the team deliver high-volume, deadline-driven client presentations without letting standards slip.

Many of the projects I lead are confidential and cannot be shown publicly. This portfolio features public work and representative case studies that capture my experience and capabilities.

The Situation

At HL, the presentations team supports bankers with highly confidential materials used in mergers and acquisitions, fundraising, and strategic communications. The work moves quickly, changes often, and requires polished client-ready execution.

My Role

As Design Manager, I now run the LA office presentation design team, after growing from leading the night team into a broader office-wide leadership role. I own the creative and operational health of designers and specialists across multiple regions, set quality standards, direct creative decisions, mentor designers across experience levels from junior to senior, and work closely with a Project Coordinator to manage intake, prioritization, and throughput across 200-400 requests per month.

The Challenge

The environment is high-stakes and unforgiving: confidential financial materials for investment banking clients, delivered under constant deadline pressure with minimal room for error. The challenge is not just volume. It is maintaining creative consistency, clear communication across regions, and individual designer growth simultaneously, without sacrificing output quality.

My Approach

I treat operational infrastructure as a creative investment. Instead of managing reactively, I build the systems that give the team clarity and confidence, then use that stability to push people creatively. The job isn't only to hold quality high and keep throughput moving. It's to challenge designers to raise their own bar, take smarter creative swings, and grow past the level they came in at. That means custom internal tools to kill repetitive work, clear onboarding and review structures so expectations are never a mystery, hands-on quality review paired with real coaching, and cross-regional communication tight enough that nothing falls through at handoff.

Key Initiatives

Creative Standards and Coaching: I hold the final quality review before work reaches clients and turn recurring issues into coaching, pushing designers to sharpen their craft and take creative risks rather than default to the safe version.

PNGMapper: A custom internal tool that automates map graphic requests, taking 1,000-plus point jobs from hours of manual work down to minutes and clearing a recurring bottleneck from the queue.

Visual Tracker: A scheduling visualizer that gives the team and coordinators real-time clarity on workload, timing, and handoffs.

Onboarding and Training: Materials that shorten ramp-up time and set shared creative standards across experience levels.

Review Structure: A consistent review process that makes growth expectations clear and gives every designer a defined path forward.

Cross-Shift Communication: Protocols that keep regional teams aligned and prevent dropped context at handoff. My team can honestly say I never surprise them and feedback never comes out of left field.

Results

Over nearly five years my scope grew from leading the night team to running the entire LA office, a direct vote of confidence in how the team performs under pressure. That team absorbs 200 to 400 confidential, deadline-driven requests a month across regions without letting quality slip, and I've retained every designer I lead in an environment known for burnout, with two more joining soon. I push the team to treat each request as a chance to make something sharper, not just clear the queue, and I hold the final quality check myself, coaching from the patterns I see so standards keep rising. The tools I built take real weight off that queue: PNGMapper turned 1,000-plus point map jobs from hours of manual work into minutes.

Building a Luxury Travel Brand from the Ground Up

I develop the visual identity, website design, and digital experience for WanderLuxe Tours, positioning the company as a premium provider of bespoke travel experiences in Egypt.

wanderluxetours.com

Deliverables

Logo design, brand identity system, color palette and typography, website design, tour page templates, responsive layouts, content structure, and inquiry forms.

The Challenge

WanderLuxe came to me with nothing but a concept: bespoke luxury travel in Egypt. No logo, no brand, no visual language. The task was to build an identity from scratch that could credibly compete in a premium market, where trust and visual sophistication are prerequisites before a client will even consider booking.

My Approach

I led every creative decision from the ground up and executed the whole thing solo: brand strategy, visual identity, and full website design and build. The direction had to feel genuinely luxurious without turning cold, and culturally grounded without being decorative. I developed a cohesive system of logo, color, and typography, then carried it through a fully responsive site with tour pages, itinerary templates, and inquiry flows.

Results

The brand and site launched, remain live and active, and have driven a large increase in bookings, turning inquiries into trips and giving WanderLuxe a credible premium presence in the luxury travel space. Strategy to shipped, revenue-generating product, built by one designer from concept to live site.

Creator, Game Designer, Art Director, Developer

Designing & Building an Original Interactive Experience with Branding Built from the Ground Up

Thunder in the Territories is an original game I conceived, designed, and am building myself. I designed the branding and visual communication, not just the game. You play the booker of a classic wrestling promotion, engineering the gimmicks, feuds, and angles that send a crowd home happy.

thunderwebsite.netlify.app

The Concept

Every wrestling promotion lives or dies on story, so this game puts you in the booker's chair, pulling the strings behind the scenes rather than stepping into the ring. Bright, colorful, and addictive, it drops you into the late 70s and early 80s territory era with pixel art that feels nostalgic without feeling muddy and fun without feeling shallow.

The Challenge

Building a game solo means owning every layer at once. The visual identity had to evoke the 16-bit console era honestly while staying vivid and readable. The systems had to turn something as human as crowd heat and booking instinct into mechanics that are actually fun to play. And because the entire cast, promotion, and championship are invented from scratch, the world had to feel specific without leaning on real names, promotions, or likenesses.

My Approach

I treated it the way I treat any brand build, starting from identity and letting it drive everything. I set an art direction rooted in territory-era artifacts, arena posters, marquee type, and vintage TV graphics, then executed the pixel art with tight palettes, chunky readable sprites, deliberate spotlight, and haze lighting. From there I designed the interface and underlying systems so the booker fantasy genuinely plays, then built it in code.

What I Built

Original game concept and design, full art direction and pixel-art execution, an invented cast of wrestlers, an original promotion identity, UI and interface design, and the working build itself. One coherent world, made solo, from first sketch to a game you can play.

Founder, Brand Designer, UI Designer, Developer

Building a Presentation Automation Product from Concept to Launch

I founded Master Template and owned the full product arc: brand identity, UI design, Next.js marketing site, and campaign materials, using AI-assisted workflows to compress a multi-person production timeline into a solo operation.

getmastertemplate.com

The Situation

Presentation templates are one of the most universally painful problems in professional design. Inconsistent formatting, manual updates, brand drift, and hours lost to repetitive production work compound across every team that relies on them. Master Template was built to solve that through automation, giving teams a faster, cleaner, and more consistent way to produce on-brand PowerPoint presentations at scale.

My Role

As founder, I owned the complete arc from initial conception to launch. That meant defining the product vision, building the brand identity, designing the UI in Figma, developing the marketing site in Next.js, and producing campaign materials in Adobe Creative Suite, all accelerated through AI-assisted workflows that compressed a multi-person production timeline into a solo operation without sacrificing quality or consistency.

The Challenge

Design software is a crowded, skeptical market. A new tool needs to communicate its value instantly and look like it belongs alongside established products. The brand needed to feel authoritative and polished without being cold, clean and bold enough to signal professionalism, but human enough to earn trust with a mixed audience of designers, marketers, and entrepreneurs.

My Approach

I developed a clean, business-forward visual identity that prioritizes clarity and confidence. The aesthetic direction strips away decoration and lets the product logic speak. Sharp typography, structured layouts, and a restrained color system work across product UI, marketing, and campaign contexts without losing consistency. Every decision was made with the full production pipeline in mind, from how the brand would render in Figma components to how it would translate into Next.js on the live site.

Results

Master Template launched as a live active product with a fully realized brand system spanning product identity, UI direction, marketing site, and campaign materials. The entire production pipeline from conception through brand strategy, design execution, development, and launch was completed by one person, combining deep domain expertise, technical proficiency across Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, and Next.js, and AI-assisted workflows that made solo founder-level output genuinely competitive.
